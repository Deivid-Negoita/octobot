◢◤ OCTOBOT IK WORKBENCH — OPERATION

STARTUPThe octobot loads and rigs itself: eight legs (LEG-1…8), each a hip-yaw → shoulder-pitch → knee-pitch chain on the real servo axes, with every mesh bound to its link.
JOINTS 1Click a leg in the list or a joint in the scene to select it. The inspector shows that servo's type, hinge axis and sweep limits. The JOINTS list streams live position and angle for every joint.
SOLVE 2Drag the cyan target. That leg solves toward it in real time and the servo angles update live.
GAIT SpacePick a pattern and heading, then press WALK to drive all eight legs. STOP restores the pose. SHOW RIG BONES reveals the skeleton inside the mesh.
COLLISIONPoses that would drive a bone through the chassis, another bone, or the floor are rejected. The leg freezes at its last safe pose, the offending bones flash red, and STATUS reads COLLISION.
EXPORTDownloads the rig and the current servo angles as JSON for the real controller.
CAMERADrag to orbit, wheel to zoom, right-drag to pan. F re-frames the robot, P hides the panels for a clear view.
Press Esc to close.
