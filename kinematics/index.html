Octobot IK workbench

StartupThe octobot loads and rigs itself: eight legs, each a hip-yaw → shoulder-pitch → knee-pitch chain on the real servo axes, with every mesh bound to the link it is screwed to.
Joints 1Click a leg in the list or a joint in the scene to select it. The inspector shows that servo's type, hinge axis and sweep limit. The joint list streams live position and angle.
Solve 2Drag the target. That leg solves toward it in real time and the servo angles update live.
Gait SpacePick a pattern and heading, then Walk to drive all eight legs. Stop restores the pose. Show rig bones reveals the skeleton inside the mesh.
CollisionPoses that would drive a link through the chassis, another link, or the floor are rejected. The leg holds its last safe pose and the offending links turn red.
ExportDownloads the rig and the current servo angles as JSON for the controller.
CameraDrag to orbit, wheel to zoom, right-drag to pan. F re-frames the robot, P hides the panels.
Press Esc to close.
